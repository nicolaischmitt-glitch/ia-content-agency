# Business Context

## Business

AI-powered content and advertising service for businesses.

## What We Do

We help businesses create high-engaging content and high-converting ads with AI at scale.

Our core capabilities include:

- AI content
- AI videos
- AI avatars

## Core Services

### 1. AI Advertising

We create AI-powered advertising creatives designed to generate sales and improve advertising performance.

### 2. AI Content Creation

We handle the ideation, production, and publishing of AI-powered content for businesses' social media channels.

### 3. AI Avatar

Our services normally include an AI avatar based on the business owner's likeness and identity. This allows the business to create content without the owner having to constantly record themselves.

For e-commerce businesses, the creative approach may be different. This is not a current focus.

### 4. Media Buying

We can manage the client's advertising campaigns, including campaign setup, implementation, optimization, and ongoing performance management.

## Main Objective

Our objective is to help businesses increase the quality and volume of their video content and advertising without requiring the business owner to constantly invest their own time, attention, and effort into manually creating content.

We aim to create advertising that performs commercially and generates a strong return on advertising spend, while reducing the traditional costs and effort associated with producing high-quality video content.

The system should allow businesses to produce significantly more content and advertising without requiring the owner to constantly record themselves or spend thousands of dollars on production setups.

## Production Philosophy

Our content should prioritize:

- High visual quality
- Photorealism
- Natural and authentic results
- High engagement
- Strong conversion potential
- Consistent visual identity
- Efficient AI-assisted production
- Scalable content production
