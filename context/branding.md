# Brand Identity

## Color Palette

The brand currently uses a minimal three-color palette designed around a high-contrast, modern and energetic visual identity.

### Primary Color — Neon Lime

- **Name:** Neon Lime
- **HEX:** `#CBF85A`
- **RGB:** `203, 248, 90`
- **Role:** Primary brand color
- **Usage:** Highlights, accents, important words, buttons, graphics, icons and visual elements that need emphasis.

### Secondary Color — Black

- **Name:** Black
- **HEX:** `#000000`
- **RGB:** `0, 0, 0`
- **Role:** Primary background color
- **Usage:** Backgrounds, dark sections, contrast areas and premium/minimal visual compositions.

### Secondary Color — White

- **Name:** White
- **HEX:** `#FFFFFF`
- **RGB:** `255, 255, 255`
- **Role:** Primary text color
- **Usage:** Headlines, body text, labels and elements placed over dark backgrounds.

## Core Brand Palette

| Color | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Neon Lime | `#CBF85A` | Primary brand color |
| Black | `#000000` | Primary background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary text |

## Visual Direction

The brand should maintain a **high-contrast, minimal and modern aesthetic**, primarily combining:

**Black + White + Neon Lime**

Neon Lime should be used strategically as the distinctive brand accent rather than overwhelming the overall design.

The visual identity should feel:

- Modern
- Bold
- Minimal
- High-contrast
- Energetic
- Digital
- Premium
